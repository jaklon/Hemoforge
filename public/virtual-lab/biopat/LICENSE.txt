HemoForge BioPAT-inspired teaching models

Original geometry and locally generated/shared textures:
HemoForge project contributors, dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No vendor CAD, logos, product photography, paid assets or add-ons are included.
These are illustrative teaching models, not verified manufacturer replicas.
Product names and trademarks remain with their respective owners.

The extra screens are HemoForge virtual readouts. Instrument values and QC are
provided at runtime; no measurement values or animations are baked in Blender.
Editable sources, rebuild scripts and reference details: assets/blender/README.md.
