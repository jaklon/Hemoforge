HemoForge bioprocess laboratory

Original geometry, lettering and locally generated textures are dedicated by
HemoForge project contributors under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No downloaded models, HDRIs, vendor CAD, logos, paid assets or add-ons.
The epoxy texture is original seeded 512px pixel data. The steel roughness
shares the project's original local brushed-roughness.png texture.

Supporting equipment is static illustrative scenery. Its screens and gauges
do not claim real measurements, certification, calibration or control.
Live BIOSTAT, Trace and ViaMass readings are separate runtime interfaces.

Editable Blender source and repeatable builder: assets/blender/README.md.
